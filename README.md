# Meralco Bills Analysis

**Context:** During the 2022 lockdown period, meter readings for my household were not regularly collected. As a result, some bills were delayed and accumulated into later billing periods. This explains the unusually high bill in May of 2022 and the missing records around this period. The data for 2026 is also limited from January to July. These circumstances were considered during the interpretation of data.

**Cost per kWh** The cost per kWh was derived from the overall amount due divided by the kWh consumption.

---

## This analysis aims to answer the questions

**1. Does my cost per kWh appear to be increasing or decreasing?**

: &emsp; &emsp;Based on the yearly average of the cost per kWh, the data shows an overall upward trend from 2022 to 2026. The average cost increased from ₱7.47/kWh in 2022 to ₱14.25/kWh in 2026. Although the monthly costs per kWh shows some fluctuations, the yearly average still increased each year.
